I am Linkai Peng (彭琳凯), a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/), advised by Prof. [Ulas Bagci](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9LUdPM4AAAAJ). 

Before joining Northwestern, I earned my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology at [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/), where I worked with Prof. [Xiaoying Tang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tQBqcr0AAAAJ).

I work on computer vision and multimodal learning, with a focus on 3D medical imaging and video understanding. My recent work includes predicting bronchoscopic accessibility from chest CT and evaluating how medical image segmentation models generalize across MRI sequences.

**I am seeking Summer 2027 research internships in computer vision, multimodal learning, and medical AI.** [CV](/files/CV.pdf)
